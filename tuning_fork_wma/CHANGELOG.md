### Added

* `TuningFork.Wma`: decodes xWMA (WMA v2, mono and stereo) to signed 16-bit PCM in pure
  Elixir, as a `TuningFork.Xwma.Decoder` that `TuningFork.Xwma.decode/1` uses without
  configuration.
